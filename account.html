What belongs in a profile?

Only information that helps coach the athlete more clearly, track progress honestly, and reduce the need to start from zero every session.

  • 1Goals and current priorities
  • 2Session and feedback history
  • 3Earned and upcoming badges
  • 4Mechanics and workload notes

Built for athletes and coaches

Players can track development. Parents can manage a youth profile. Coaches can organize team problems, practice priorities, and consultation notes.

Privacy before features

Production accounts should use secure authentication, role-based access, guardian controls for minors, and database policies that prevent profiles from being viewed by the wrong person.
